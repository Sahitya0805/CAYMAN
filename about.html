A VISION THAT STARTED BEFORE COVID

The story of Cayman Super Kings began with a vision shared by a few passionate individuals who believed that cricket could bring together a unique community on the Cayman Islands. Before COVID-19 changed the world, Gopal, Majin Paul, Christopher Balraj and Kiruba had already envisioned building a team made up of individuals who had moved to the Cayman Islands to pursue careers, build new lives, and grow professionally—but who also shared a deep love for cricket.

When life gradually returned to normal, the founders picked up where they had left off. In 2022, Cayman Super Kings was officially founded, bringing together players from different cricketing backgrounds. CSK played its first tournament in early 2023 and has continued competing ever since.

From the beginning, the team was built around more than just cricket. It was about creating a community—bringing people together, developing players, creating friendships, and giving talented individuals an opportunity to compete and improve.

Year-by-Year Journey

Club History & Milestones

Pre-COVID The Vision

A Vision Conceived

Gopal, Majin Paul, Christopher Balraj, and Kiruba envision building a team for professionals in Grand Cayman who share a deep love for cricket.

2022 Foundation

The Birth of CSK

Cayman Super Kings is officially founded under the leadership of Christopher Balraj; Rahul Garg joins towards the end of 2022.

2023 Debut & Bermuda

Tournament Debut & World Cup Qualifier

CSK plays its first competitive tournament. Christopher Balraj represents the Cayman Islands national team in Bermuda for the World Cup Qualifiers.

2024 Leadership & Global

Captaincy Handover & International T10

Rahul Garg takes over captaincy and represents CSK in international T10 leagues playing alongside David Warner, Carlos Brathwaite, and Alex Hales.

2025 Knockouts

T10 Knockouts & Dual International T10

CSK qualifies for T10 knockouts under Rahul Garg. Chris Balraj joins international T10 under Shabaz Nadeem; Rahul and Gokul join National Training Squad.

2026+ 🚀 THE JOURNEY CONTINUES

Developing the Next Generation

Providing pathways for school cricketers to train and compete alongside senior players, growing Cayman cricket for the future.
