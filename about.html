Our Heritage

About Cayman Super Kings

From humble beginnings in George Town to lifting the CICA T20 Championship — built on discipline, unity, and community passion.

Origin Story

Forged in Grand Cayman

Founded in 2023 by a dedicated cohort of local cricket enthusiasts, Cayman Super Kings (CSK) was established to bring professional discipline, high athletic standards, and inclusive community cricket to the Cayman Islands.

Embodying the iconic yellow and navy spirit, CSK rapidly progressed from Division 2 contenders into premier Division 1 titleholders, fostering deep roots within the Cayman sporting ecosystem.

Club Values:

Integrity in competition, ruthless tactical preparation, uncompromised youth protection, and lifelong camaraderie on and off the field.

Year-by-Year Journey

2023 – 2026 Club Timeline

2023 Foundation

Club Inception & First Squad

Formed in George Town. Entered CICA Division 2 and achieved promotion in the inaugural campaign.

2024 Growth

First Major Final & Youth Launch

Reached Division 1 40-Over final and established grassroots academy for Under-15 Caymanian players.

2025 Silverware

T20 Super League Champions

Lifted the premier T20 trophy with an undefeated postseason run, establishing CSK as the gold standard.

2026 🔥 CURRENT STREAK (8-0)

Undefeated League Run & Academy Expansion

Unbeaten across both T20 and 40-Over formats, expanding the senior and youth development roster to 22 athletes.

The Fortress

Jimmy Powell Oval

Located along West Bay Road in George Town, Jimmy Powell Oval is the home ground of the Cayman Super Kings. Renowned for its lively true bounce and picturesque Caribbean palm backdrop.

Ground Key Facts:

  • Capacity: 1,200 Spectators (Free Admission on League Days)
  • Surface: Natural Turf Pitch & Bermuda Grass Outfield
  • Facilities: Two-bay practice nets, pavilion, changing rooms, floodlights
  • Location: West Bay Road, George Town, Grand Cayman
