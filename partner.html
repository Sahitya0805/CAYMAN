Frequently Asked Questions

Partnership FAQs

1. How are sponsorship funds utilized by the club?

100% of sponsorship revenue goes directly into player safety gear, match fees, umpire certifications, pitch hire at Jimmy Powell Oval, and subsidising free coaching equipment for Under-19 youth academy participants.

2. Can partnership packages be customised to our marketing objectives?

Yes. While our standard tiers (Premier, Club, Community) provide transparent structured benefits, our executive committee is eager to co-create bespoke corporate hospitality, employee cricket clinics, or CSR activations.

3. When does the sponsorship agreement take effect?

Agreements run across the entire CICA calendar year covering both the Autumn T20 Super League and the Spring 40-Over Cup, with kit production locking in 4 weeks prior to opening fixture dates.
