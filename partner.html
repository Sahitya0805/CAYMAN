COMMERCIAL ALLIANCES

Power the Pride of Cayman Cricket

Partner with Cayman Super Kings Cricket Club and support local cricket excellence — on the field and in the community.

What your sponsorship delivers

2027 Season Deliverables

Season-long brand visibility

  • Your logo on all 22 match jerseys, worn at every fixture across the 2027 season
  • Named sponsor credit in every post-match feature on Instagram @caymansuperkings — 12+ fixtures a year, every match covered
  • Ground banner presence at our home fixtures (Jimmy Powell Oval & Smith Road Oval)
  • Logo and link on this website — every page, all season
  • Co-branded moments: season jersey reveal post, season launch announcement, trophy celebrations

Real community & youth impact

  • Direct support for our development squad — 3–4 Caymanian and resident youth players (U18/U22) who train and play alongside the senior team: coaching, equipment, and match opportunities
  • Free, family-friendly matches that bring together players and supporters of many nationalities, year-round
  • Your brand aligned with sport, youth development, and active healthy lifestyles in the Cayman Islands

Engagement for your people

  • An invitation for your staff to a CSK match day or a friendly corporate cricket session with the squad
  • Player appearances for your community or company events, by arrangement

Full accountability

  • An end-of-season impact report: how funds were used, fixtures played, social reach, youth development progress, and a photo record of your brand in action
  • A single point of contact on our committee throughout the season

Where your funds go

  • Team kit — match jerseys and caps for the 22-player squad, carrying your brand
  • Competition — registration fees for T20 and 35-over league and tournament play
  • Match operations — ground fees, match balls, and equipment for year-round fixtures
  • Youth development — training, coaching support, and equipment for our U18/U22 development players
  • Media & promotion — match-day photography and the post-match features your brand appears in
Every dollar goes to club activities. No funds are paid to individuals.
