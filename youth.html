OUR PHILOSOPHY

Building More Than Cricketers. Building Champions For Life.

At Cayman Super Kings, cricket is more than boundaries and wickets—it is an extraordinary classroom for character, resilience, discipline, and lifelong friendships. Our Junior Academy was established to give every young athlete in Grand Cayman the platform, technical guidance, and encouragement needed to discover their full sporting potential.

Whether a child is holding an English willow bat for the very first time or refining an inswinging yorker with certified CICA coaches, we provide a structured, age-appropriate curriculum. Sessions combine biomechanically sound fundamentals with high-energy games, ensuring that every session is both technically rewarding and immensely fun.

Through regular match exposure at George Town’s iconic Smith Road Oval, junior players learn strategic game awareness, loud communication between wickets, sportsmanship in victory and defeat, and the proud tradition of the 22 yards.

🎯 Technical Mastery

Front-foot strokeplay, repeatable bowling actions, and reflex catching.

🏃 Athletic Fitness

Agility ladders, sprint mechanics, and core balance drills.

🤝 Sportsmanship

Respect for teammates, umpires, and opponents at every step.

🛡️ Safety & Inclusivity

Strict child welfare protocols and small coach-to-student ratios.

STRUCTURED PATHWAYS

Age Divisions & Training Stages

Tailored learning stages designed to meet each age group at their exact developmental phase, from first swings to competitive island leagues.

DIVISION 01

Little Kings

Under-9 (Ages 5 to 9)

An exhilarating introduction to cricket emphasizing joy, reaction drills, softball hand-eye games, batting swings, and athletic movement.

  • Soft cricket balls & lightweight safety gear
  • Throwing, catching & agility games
  • Confidence-building team atmosphere
DIVISION 02

Rising Kings

Under-13 (Ages 10 to 13)

The core developmental bridge where players transition to hard-ball leather cricket, full batting pads, turf net practice, and junior league derbies.

  • Front & back foot strokeplay mechanics
  • Pace run-up alignment & spin variations
  • Junior CICA League fixtures
DIVISION 03

Academy XI

Under-17 (Ages 14 to 17)

Elite high-performance training with video analysis, powerplay tactics, match situational pressure drills, and a direct pipeline into the senior CSK squad.

  • Match scenario batting (Powerplay & Death)
  • High-intensity strength & stamina drills
  • Direct pathway to Senior CSK League squad
