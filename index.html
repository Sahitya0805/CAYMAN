CSK CHRONICLES
CHAPTER 01 PRE-COVID — 2022

A Vision That Started Before COVID

The story of Cayman Super Kings began with a vision shared by passionate individuals who believed cricket could unite a unique community. Before COVID-19 changed the world, Gopal, Majin Paul, Christopher Balraj, and Kiruba envisioned building a team of professionals moving to Grand Cayman.

When life returned to normal in 2022, Cayman Super Kings was officially founded, bringing together players from diverse cricketing backgrounds to embrace competitive leather-ball cricket.

✦ Pre-COVID vision conceived by Gopal, Majin Paul, Christopher Balraj & Kiruba
✦ 2022 Official Club Founding & Rahul Garg joins squad

CHAPTER 02 2023

Tournament Debut & Bermuda Qualifiers

CSK played its inaugural competitive tournament in early 2023, competing with fierce passion. Under the on-field leadership of Christopher Balraj and managerial guidance of Majin Paul, the club quickly established its competitive identity.

Christopher Balraj achieved a historic milestone by earning selection for the Cayman Islands national team and travelling to Bermuda for the Sub-Regional World Cup Qualifiers.

✦ First official competitive tournament campaign
✦ Chris Balraj represents Cayman in Bermuda World Cup Qualifiers

CHAPTER 03 2024

Leadership & International T10

After two formative years leading from the front, Christopher Balraj passed the captaincy to Rahul Garg. Rahul took on the responsibility of guiding CSK into its next chapter of athletic excellence and tactical structure.

Rahul Garg took the next step in his cricket journey, playing in an international T10 league alongside legends David Warner, Carlos Brathwaite, Alex Hales, Thisara Perera, Beau Webster, and Shakib Al Hasan.

✦ Rahul Garg assumes CSK club captaincy
✦ International T10 league debut under captain David Warner

CHAPTER 04 2025

Underdogs to Knockout Contenders

Being the underdog became part of the CSK identity. In 2025, relentless training produced one of the club's greatest moments: qualifying for the knockout stages of the T10 tournament under skipper Rahul Garg.

Christopher Balraj was also selected for the international T10 league under Shabaz Nadeem alongside Rahul, while Rahul and Gokul Krishnan both joined the Cayman Islands national team training squad.

✦ Qualification for T10 Tournament Knockout Stages
✦ Rahul & Gokul selected for National Team Training Squad

CHAPTER 05 2026 & BEYOND

Developing the Next Generation

Young players from local schools have started training and playing alongside senior CSK players, experiencing a competitive environment from an early age and developing skills, confidence, and discipline.

What makes CSK special is the journey. From an idea before COVID to an ambitious club where new milestones are achieved each season: the graph is only going up.

✦ School cricket pathway training alongside senior players
✦ Continuous progression towards higher competitive cricket
