THE ARCHIVES

OUR JOURNEY

Turn the pages to explore the historic legacy, milestones, and championship chapters of Cayman Super Kings.

CSK CHRONICLES
CHAPTER 01 2018 — 2020

The Genesis on Grand Cayman

Founded by passionate cricketers and community leaders in George Town, Cayman Super Kings rose from weekend pitch scrimmages at Jimmy Powell Oval into a disciplined sporting collective.

United under the iconic canary yellow and royal blue colours, the founding roster trained under tropical sunbeams with one steadfast ambition: bringing high-octane championship cricket to the Cayman Islands.

✦ Inaugural squad formation of 16 founding players
✦ Affiliation with the Cayman Islands Cricket Association
ARCHIVE 2018
Founding Matches 18 Wins / 22 MP

George Town, Grand Cayman · The Founding Squad

CHAPTER 02 2021 — 2022

The Inaugural Silverware

A year etched in gold. In our third competitive season, CSK mounted a historic 12-match winning run across the CICA Division 1 Championship, outclassing seasoned opponents with explosive batting and disciplined death bowling.

The thrilling final at Smith Road Oval crowned Cayman Super Kings as island champions, sealing our reputation as the most electrifying cricket force in the territory.

✦ CICA Division 1 Championship Winners
✦ Highest team total of the tournament (248/4 in 20 overs)
CHAMPIONS
Win Percentage 88.5% Season

Smith Road Oval · Division 1 Trophy Presentation

CHAPTER 03 2023 — 2024

Grassroots & Youth Academy

True champions build legacies that endure. In early 2023, the club formally launched the CSK Youth Cricket Academy, welcoming over 200 young Caymanian girls and boys to learn world-class cricket fundamentals.

Through certified coaching clinics, weekend junior leagues, and school outreach programs, CSK continues to cultivate the next generation of international cricket talent in the Caribbean.

✦ Over 200+ active youth academy students registered
✦ 4 Junior graduates promoted directly into Senior squad
ACADEMY
Youth Trained 200+ Cadets

CSK Training Complex · Youth Development Camp

CHAPTER 04 2025

Dominating the T20 Circuit

A relentless display of athletic mastery and tactical precision. In 2025, Cayman Super Kings conquered the Premier T20 Cup with an undefeated clean sheet, shattering multiple league batting records.

With signature power hitting, razor-sharp fielding, and the unwavering roar of yellow-clad fans in the stands, CSK set a new benchmark for Caribbean domestic cricket excellence.

✦ Undefeated Champions of the 2025 Premier T20 Cup
✦ Most 6s hit & Best Economy rate in the entire tournament
DOMINANCE
Cup Record 10 - 0 Unbeaten

National Stadium · T20 Premier Grand Final

CHAPTER 05 2026 & BEYOND

Global Ambitions & Beyond

The saga continues to expand beyond our Caribbean shores. With international touring fixtures scheduled across Florida, Jamaica, and Barbados, CSK is stepping onto the global stage.

Backed by world-class partners and our passionate Whistle Podu community worldwide, we remain dedicated to our core motto: One Team, One Dream, Infinite Passion.

✦ North American & Caribbean Invitational Series debut
✦ Construction of new state-of-the-art training turf nets
